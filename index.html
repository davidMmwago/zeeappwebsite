Zee

A personal study planner for Android tablets

Zee is a private, single-user study app. It keeps lecture slides and handwritten notes organised by module, and includes cue cards, a focus timer, a to-do planner, a simple budget planner and study stats.

If you choose to connect a Google account, Zee can:

  • show your Google Calendar events and add your to-dos to the calendar,
  • turn PowerPoint files into PDFs using your Google Drive,
  • back up your study files to a private app folder in your Google Drive.

Zee is not sold or distributed publicly.
